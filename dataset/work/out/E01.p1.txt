--- reference
ROAS, return on ad spend, is the revenue attributed to your ads divided by what you spent on them. A 3.8x result means every unit of currency spent on advertising brought back 3.8 units of attributed revenue. When you present it, say that it is revenue rather than profit, since margins and other costs are not included in the ratio.
