Start with the pod list to see its status, then pull the logs from the container's previous run.
```dyui
{"ui":"code","code":"kubectl get pods checkout-api-7d9f\nkubectl logs checkout-api-7d9f --previous","language":"bash"}
```
A CrashLoopBackOff status plus a restart count that keeps climbing confirms the loop, and the previous-run logs usually name the reason it dies.
