--- reference
Setting a customer's plan to "basic" is idempotent: repeating it any number of times leaves the plan at basic. Adding a charge is not, because every repeat creates another charge, which is why payment calls often carry a unique key so retries don't bill twice.

=== J-0019
--- user
what's a canary deploy?
--- reference
A canary deploy sends a new version to a small slice of traffic first while everyone else stays on the old one. If the metrics look healthy you widen the rollout, and if not you roll back with limited impact.

=== J-0007
--- user
what does an index do in a db? need to explain it to a new agent
--- reference
An index lets the database jump straight to the matching rows instead of scanning the whole table, much like the index at the back of a book. The tradeoff is extra storage and slightly slower writes.
--- derived

=== J-0076
--- user
monolith or microservices for our 5-person startup? be honest
--- reference
Start with a monolith. With five people, the coordination cost of many services (deploys, networking, debugging across boundaries) outweighs the benefits, and you can split a piece out later once a real bottleneck appears.
--- derived
