=== J-0020
--- user
Today's escalation count came in at 10 and my manager asked whether that is a lot. What should I tell her?
--- reference
Ten escalations means little on its own, because whether it is high depends on your usual daily count and on how many tickets came in. Compare it with the recent daily average before calling it a problem, and if it sits above that, look at what the escalated tickets have in common.

=== J-0010
--- user
Should dark mode be the default on our shop-floor tablets, or should it follow whatever the device is set to?
--- reference
Following the device setting is usually the better default, because it respects what each person already chose and avoids surprising anyone. The exception is a dim or glare-prone workshop, where a dark default can be easier on the eyes, so test on a few tablets in real lighting before deciding.

=== J-0071
--- user
In your view, is remote work better for small teams? We are about eight people and debating going fully remote.
--- reference
It tends to work well for small teams that already communicate clearly in writing, since there is less overhead to coordinate and more freedom in hiring. The risk is losing informal contact, so a small team that goes remote should plan regular calls and an occasional meetup to keep that connection.

=== J-0060
--- user
Pourriez-vous m'expliquer ce qu'est un feature flag ? Mon équipe en parle beaucoup et je n'ai pas osé demander.
--- reference
Un feature flag est un interrupteur dans le code qui permet d'activer ou de désactiver une fonctionnalité sans redéployer l'application. Il sert à lancer une nouveauté progressivement, à la tester auprès d'un petit groupe ou à la couper rapidement en cas de problème.
--- derived


=== J-0052
--- user
Che cosa significa "churn rate"? Lo leggo spesso nei report ma non sono sicuro.
--- reference
Il churn rate è la percentuale di clienti che smettono di usare un servizio, o di acquistare, in un certo periodo. Si calcola dividendo i clienti persi nel periodo per quelli presenti all'inizio, e un valore basso indica che i clienti restano.
--- derived


=== J-0046
--- user
so when would I use one over polling?
--- reference
Use a webhook when events are infrequent or unpredictable and you want to react right away, such as a new inquiry arriving or a listing status changing. Polling makes more sense when the other system offers no callbacks, or when you need to control exactly when and how often data is pulled.
--- derived
