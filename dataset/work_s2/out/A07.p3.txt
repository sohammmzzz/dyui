=== A-0139
--- user
hi, I'd like a quote for the project. I'll give you my company name, email, a budget between 100 and 5000 in steps of 100 (required), and a start date. The button should say Get quote.
--- reference
Four fields are enough to request your quote.
```dyui
{"ui":"form","fields":[{"name":"company","label":"Company"},{"name":"email","label":"Email","type":"email"},{"name":"budget","label":"Budget ($)","type":"slider","required":true,"min":100,"max":5000,"step":100},{"name":"start_date","label":"Start date","type":"date"}],"submit_label":"Get quote","action":"request_quote"}
```
Only the budget is required, and submitting sends the details back to me.
--- derived
