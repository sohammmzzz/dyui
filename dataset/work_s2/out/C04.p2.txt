=== C-0098
--- user
Please write SQL that counts this month's orders by status, highest count first, from a table called orders with columns status and created_at. In addition, advise on the customer wording for this: check-in wait is at 18 min, above the 14 min threshold, review before Friday.
--- reference
Grouping by status and sorting on the count gives the monthly breakdown.
```dyui
{"ui":"code","code":"SELECT status, COUNT(*) AS n\nFROM orders\nWHERE created_at >= date_trunc('month', now())\nGROUP BY status\nORDER BY n DESC;","language":"sql"}
```
For the customer, the wording below fits.
```dyui
{"ui":"callout","text":"Check-in wait is at 18 min, above the 14 min threshold. Review it before Friday.","level":"warning"}
```
`date_trunc('month', now())` resets the window on the first of each month.
