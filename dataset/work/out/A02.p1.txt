=== A-0032

--- reference
Here is the Orion Dashboard as a card with a booking action.
```dyui
{"ui":"card","title":"Orion Dashboard","subtitle":"Free cancellation","children":[{"ui":"stat","label":"Active learners","value":"5,736"}],"actions":[{"label":"Book","action":"book_item","style":"primary"}]}
```
The Book button sends its action back to me when a student presses it.
