=== J-0097
--- user
Could you clarify the distinction between an SLO and an SLA? I need to describe it accurately in a client-facing report.
--- reference
An SLO is a target a team sets for itself, such as how available a service should be, while an SLA is the formal promise made to customers, often with penalties or credits if it is missed. In a client report, quote the SLA as the commitment and the SLO as the internal goal that gives you a safety margin.

=== J-0001
--- user
Pouvez-vous m'expliquer ce que signifie le taux de désabonnement, ou churn rate ? Je dois l'utiliser dans un dossier d'indemnisation et je veux être sûr de la définition.
--- reference
Le churn rate est la proportion de clients, ou de contrats, perdus sur une période donnée. On le calcule en divisant le nombre de clients perdus par le nombre de clients présents au début de la période, et plus il est bas, meilleure est la fidélisation.
--- derived

=== J-0050
--- user
nps - what does it measure
--- reference
NPS, or Net Promoter Score, measures how likely customers are to recommend you, based on a single "would you recommend us" question. The score is the share of promoters minus the share of detractors.
