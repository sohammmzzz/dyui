Hold on, before I tap anything: what exactly does ARPDAU measure? I want to be sure I'm reading the $0.68 correctly.
--- reference
ARPDAU stands for average revenue per daily active user: the revenue earned in a day divided by the number of players who were active that day. So the $0.68 is the average amount each active player generated on that day, spread across payers and non-payers alike. It says nothing by itself about whether that is high or low, because I have no target or earlier values in this conversation to compare it with.
